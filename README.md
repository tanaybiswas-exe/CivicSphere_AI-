🛡️ নাগরিক বন্ধু (NagorikBondhu) • CivicSphere AIAI-Powered Citizen Assistance, Civic Grievance Drafting & Public Transparency Platform for BangladeshAn independent civic-tech initiative bridging the gap between everyday citizens and public service delivery in Bangladesh through Bangla-first AI, geospatial deduplication, and transparent cost auditing.📌 Problem StatementMillions of citizens in Bangladesh face everyday civic challenges—damaged roads, waterlogging, uncollected waste, broken streetlights, consumer overcharging, and bureaucratic complexity—yet struggle with:Drafting Formal Complaints: Not knowing which government authority or legal framework applies to a specific civic issue.Fragmented Tracking: Losing track of complaint progress, updates, and formal copies after drafting.Duplicate Reporting: Multiple uncoordinated reports for the exact same street-level hazard without priority aggregation.Public Cost & Rights Opacity: Difficulty verifying local infrastructure Bill of Quantities (BOQ) against standard LGED/PWD rates or calculating statutory consumer rights rewards.Dense Policy Documents: Navigating lengthy government PDF circulars to extract actionable instructions.💡 Our Solutionনাগরিক বন্ধু (NagorikBondhu / CivicSphere AI) unifies civic grievance drafting, geospatial hazard mapping, public expenditure auditing, and Bangla AI guidance into a single, mobile-responsive web application.Transparency Disclaimer: NagorikBondhu is an independent citizen assistance and civic-tech prototype. It is not an official government portal and does not claim direct API submission to government ministries.✨ Key FeaturesModuleCapability & Impact🏠 Home DashboardDisplays live KPI counters (Total, Pending, In Progress, Resolved), recent complaint previews, and an area-wise hazard mini-map.✍️ AI Complaint GeneratorTakes district, area, category, landmark, photo/file evidence, and Bangla/English problem descriptions to automatically identify the responsible authority, cite relevant Bangladeshi laws, and generate an editable, downloadable, and printable formal complaint letter.📋 Smart Complaint TrackerAssigns unique tracking IDs (NB-2026-XXXX), tracks lifecycle stages (Draft → Submitted → In Progress → Resolved), and maintains a timestamped status history modal.🗺️ Geospatial Issue MapInteractive Leaflet map with category/status filters, click-to-pin coordinates, 100-meter Haversine duplicate detection, and community Upvoting to surface high-priority hazards.💬 Bangla AI Citizen AssistantConversational assistant answering civic and public-service queries in Bangla/English with verified official government portal links (bdris.gov.bd, grs.gov.bd, bangladesh.gov.bd).📄 PDF Document AssistantClient-side PDF.js extraction paired with AI/keyword Q&A to retrieve grounded references directly from uploaded government circulars and forms without hallucination.💰 Public Project BOQ AuditCompares contractor quoted unit rates against LGED and PWD schedule benchmarks, flagging items exceeding a >20% variance threshold alongside budget variance metrics.🏷️ Consumer Rights Calculator (16121)Calculates overcharge amount, percentage markup above MRP, and the statutory 25% citizen whistleblower reward under the Consumer Rights Protection Act, 2009.🛡️ Anti-Corruption & Portal DirectoryHighlights whistleblower protection (Public-Interest Information Disclosure Act, 2011), ACC helpline (106), emergency helplines (999, 333), and verified national service portals.👤
